Xbox's shot at Game of the Year

Xbox went without a Game of the Year nomination again at The Game Awards 2025 — the show went to a field led by Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, alongside Death Stranding 2, Donkey Kong Bananza, Hades 2, Hollow Knight: Silksong and Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2. But 2026 marks Xbox's 25th anniversary, and the first-party lineup finally lines up with the moment. Here's what's carrying that hope.

PLAYGROUND GAMES

Fable

The strongest GOTY case on paper — over 15 years since the last mainline entry, an open world built to react to your choices, and a studio with Forza Horizon's pedigree behind it.

PLAYGROUND GAMES

Forza Horizon 6

A new setting and, if the series' track record holds, another system-seller that critics tend to reward for sheer craft.

THE COALITION

Gears of War: E-Day

An origin story for a franchise that hasn't been this anticipated in years — a chance to reset how Gears gets talked about.

343 INDUSTRIES

Halo: Campaign Evolved

A rebuilt campaign for the game that made the brand. Nostalgia alone won't win awards, but execution might.
